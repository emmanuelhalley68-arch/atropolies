# Data Subject Request Process — ATROPOLIES

Designate a monitored privacy contact before launch.

1. Receive request through [privacy email/form].
2. Record date, request type and reference number without collecting excess data.
3. Verify the requester proportionately; do not request unnecessary ID copies.
4. Identify relevant systems and processors.
5. Assess identity, scope, legal basis, exceptions and third-party rights.
6. Provide access/correction/objection/erasure/restriction/portability response
   as applicable under law.
7. Update active systems and notify processors/recipients where required.
8. Record the decision, action, dates and reason for any refusal/limitation.
9. Securely close the request and retain only the minimum necessary record.

Do not promise a deadline until counsel confirms the applicable rules and the
company has an operational workflow. Escalate complex, sensitive or disputed
requests to the designated privacy lead/legal adviser.
