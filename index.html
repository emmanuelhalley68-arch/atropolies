Arena · VALORANT

Understanding VALORANT Settings

Guide template — verify all game-specific values and update date before publishing.

Atropolis Editorial · Beginner · 6 minute read · Updated when verified

Introduction

Settings affect how a game looks, sounds and responds to your input. There is no single sensitivity that is best for every player. Use a consistent starting point, practise, and make small changes rather than copying settings without understanding them.

Sensitivity and DPI

DPI describes mouse sensor sensitivity at the device level. In-game sensitivity changes how far your view moves in response to mouse movement. Effective sensitivity depends on both values. Test changes gradually and keep notes.

Crosshair

A crosshair should remain easy to see against different backgrounds. Choose a colour and size that you can track comfortably. Avoid claiming that one code is universally best.

Example settings checklist

SettingWhat to consider
Mouse DPIUse a consistent mouse setting
In-game sensitivityChoose a controllable range and practise
CrosshairPrioritise visibility and clarity
GraphicsBalance visibility and stable performance

FAQ

Should I copy a professional player's settings?

Use them as a reference, not a rule. Your equipment and comfort matter.

How often should I change sensitivity?

Avoid changing it constantly. Practise with one setting before deciding whether it needs adjustment.

Can I use these settings on every device?

Mouse, screen and performance differences can change the experience.

About Atropolis

Our Story — One City, Every Dream

A Kenyan digital city built around learning, entertainment, creativity and business.

How Atropolis Was Born

Atropolis Innovations is a growing vision from Dawsonville, Rift Valley, Kenya. The idea is to bring useful digital resources into one connected place: a library for learning, an arena for gaming, a cinema for stories and a tech district for building businesses.

The ambition goes beyond a website. The long-term vision includes innovation and, one day, real-world construction projects. The work begins by building useful things honestly, one step at a time.

Our Vision

To build a digital city from Kenya where anyone can learn a skill, play a game, watch a story, and build a business — and one day build real cities too.

Our Mission

  • Make knowledge and practical resources accessible.
  • Build phone-friendly experiences for Kenyan users.
  • Publish original, responsible entertainment and gaming content.
  • Help local businesses establish a digital presence.
  • Grow an innovation culture rooted in real problems.
