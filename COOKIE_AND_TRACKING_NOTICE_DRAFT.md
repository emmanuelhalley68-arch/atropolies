# Cookies, Local Storage and Tracking Notice — DRAFT

Do not publish until the live website has been scanned and every cookie, local
storage key, SDK, analytics tag, advertising tag and third-party embed is listed.

For each technology record:
- Name/vendor
- Purpose (strictly necessary, preferences, analytics, advertising, embedded media)
- Data collected
- Duration/expiry
- Whether it is first-party or third-party
- Whether it transfers data outside Kenya
- User control/withdrawal method

The authentication flow may store session information in browser storage to
keep a user signed in. Embedded video, maps, fonts or external media may contact
third-party services. Verify actual browser behaviour.

Do not load optional analytics/advertising technologies before the legally
required consent or other valid permission has been obtained. Provide a working
way to change or withdraw optional choices. Do not describe necessary session
storage as advertising tracking.
