ATROPOLIES LAUNCH DECISION

Do not launch to real customers until the responsible owner reviews and signs
this decision.

GO criteria:
[ ] No known critical or high-severity security failures.
[ ] Authentication and account recovery tested.
[ ] RLS and role permissions tested with separate accounts.
[ ] Private storage access tested.
[ ] No secrets exposed in browser code or public repository.
[ ] HTTPS and security headers verified on the live deployment.
[ ] Backups enabled as required and restore test completed.
[ ] Monitoring and incident-response process assigned.
[ ] Privacy notice, terms, support contact, and data-handling practices reviewed.
[ ] Payment functions disabled until provider integration has passed sandbox
    tests and provider-specific verification review.
[ ] Qualified independent security review completed or risks explicitly
    accepted by the accountable project owner.

Decision:
[ ] GO for limited launch
[ ] NO-GO — further work required
