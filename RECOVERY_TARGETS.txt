ATROPOLIES RECOVERY TARGETS — INITIAL DRAFT

These are planning targets, not provider guarantees. Confirm realistic targets
against the hosting plan, backup frequency, staffing and budget.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO):
How much recent data could the business tolerate losing?
Initial planning target: decide before launch; do not assume zero data loss.

Recovery Time Objective (RTO):
How long can the service be unavailable?
Initial planning target: define a realistic target after a restore drill.

For each critical service, document:
- Maximum acceptable downtime
- Maximum acceptable data loss
- Responsible recovery owner
- Required credentials and dependencies
- Steps and verification checks

Do not advertise an RTO or RPO until it has been demonstrated in restore tests.
