ATROPOLIES SECURITY MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE

WEEKLY
- Review administrator accounts and recent sign-in activity.
- Review unusual Auth/API/database activity.
- Review recent GitHub commits and Netlify deployments.
- Check for unexpected user/role/storage changes.

MONTHLY
- Review dependencies and security advisories.
- Review RLS policies and administrator access.
- Confirm backup status and available retention.
- Review environment secrets and rotate when exposure or policy requires it.
- Review open security issues and close resolved ones.

QUARTERLY
- Perform a restore drill in a separate environment.
- Review incident-response contacts and procedures.
- Re-test customer-to-customer access boundaries.
- Review payment provider integration and refund/failure flows if enabled.
- Arrange independent security testing appropriate to platform growth.

AFTER EVERY MAJOR CHANGE
- Re-test authentication, authorization, RLS, storage, and payment-related flows.
- Review the deployment diff and rollback option.
- Confirm no secrets or customer data entered the repository.
