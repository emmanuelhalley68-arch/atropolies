KENYA PRIVACY / DATA PROTECTION READINESS CHECKLIST

This is a readiness checklist, not legal advice or a compliance certificate.

[ ] Identify the legal operator and whether it is controller, processor, or both.
[ ] Map every personal-data collection, purpose, system, recipient and country.
[ ] Determine ODPC registration requirements for each role/activity.
[ ] Assess whether a Data Protection Officer must be designated.
[ ] Establish lawful basis and transparent notices for each purpose.
[ ] Minimize data and define retention/deletion periods.
[ ] Provide a route for data-subject rights requests.
[ ] Assess children/minors and sensitive data before offering such features.
[ ] Assess whether a DPIA is required or prudent for high-risk processing.
[ ] Document cross-border transfers and safeguards.
[ ] Sign appropriate processor agreements with service providers.
[ ] Maintain technical/organizational security measures and evidence.
[ ] Maintain breach detection, assessment, notification and communication steps.
[ ] Train staff and restrict access.
[ ] Review privacy notices, terms, cookie notice and internal policies.
[ ] Obtain qualified Kenyan legal/privacy review before launch.

ODPC official resources:
https://www.odpc.go.ke/
https://www.odpc.go.ke/data-protection-laws-kenya/
https://www.odpc.go.ke/rights-of-a-data-subject/

The ODPC and Data Protection Act requirements depend on the actual operator,
processing activities, exemptions, thresholds and circumstances. Do not assume
registration is optional; determine applicability with current ODPC guidance.
